3. What the app does

Powerwall Manager is a software tool that connects to your Tesla Powerwall via the Tesla Fleet API and to your Octopus Energy account via the Octopus Energy API. It provides battery monitoring, manual export controls, automated overnight charge-and-export cycles, daytime holiday export mode, and Tesla vehicle charging coordination.

The app sends commands to your Powerwall to charge from the grid, discharge and export to the grid, adjust battery reserve levels, and modify Tesla vehicle charging settings. These are real commands that cause physical changes to the operation of your hardware.
