2. What the app does

Powerwall Manager connects to your Tesla Powerwall via the Tesla Fleet API and your Octopus Energy account via the Octopus Energy API. It provides battery monitoring, manual export controls, and an automated overnight arbitrage cycle that charges and discharges your Powerwall to take advantage of variable Agile Outgoing export tariff rates.
