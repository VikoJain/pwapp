Quick start
1
Connect your Tesla account
Go to the Setup tab and tap Connect with Tesla. You'll be taken to Tesla's own login page — sign in and grant permissions. You'll be returned to the app automatically.
2
Connect Octopus Energy
On the same Setup tab, enter your Octopus API key and account number. The app detects your Agile Outgoing tariff automatically. See the Connecting your accounts card below for exactly where to find these.
3
You're ready
Tap Home to see your live battery. Tap Tariff for today's export rates. Head to Night when you're ready to set up automated overnight arbitrage.
Connecting your accounts
Tesla
Your Tesla login is handled entirely by Tesla's own page — the app never sees your password. Once connected, your authorisation token is stored securely on the server so the app can run overnight even when your phone is off.
If you need to reconnect, go to Settings → Reconnect Tesla. You may also need to reconnect if Tesla revokes access after an account security change.
Octopus Energy
You need two things from your Octopus account:
1
API key
Log in at octopus.energy → click your name top right → Account → scroll to API access. Your key starts with sk_live_
2
Account number
On the same page, your account number is in the format A-12345678. It appears near the top of the API access section.
The app requires an active Agile Outgoing export tariff. If none is detected, contact Octopus Energy to add one to your account.
Home tab
Shows your Powerwall's live status, refreshing every 30 seconds.
Battery % — current charge level. The display interpolates between API updates so the number stays live.
Grid / Home / Export stats — live power flows in kW. A negative grid value means you are exporting to the grid.
Today vs 5-day average chart — the blue line is today's battery level, the grey dashed line is your average across the same time of day over the past 5 days. Use this to judge whether you have more or less charge than usual before deciding to export manually.
Timed export — manually export for a fixed number of minutes. Useful when you spot a high rate on the Tariff tab. The server stops it automatically even if your phone locks.
Export to target % — discharges to a specific battery percentage then stops automatically. Good for precise manual control.
Battery reserve — sets Tesla's minimum backup level. At 0% the full battery is available for export. The overnight cycle manages this automatically — only adjust manually if needed.
Tariff tab
Shows your live Octopus Agile Outgoing export rates for today, and tomorrow's rates from around 4pm.
Rate chart — each bar is a 30-minute slot. Green = best (>25p), blue = good (18–25p), amber = fair (<18p).
Best export windows — the top 5 slots ranked by rate. Use this to plan manual exports or to sense-check whether the overnight cycle is running at the right time.
Earnings estimator — enter a kWh figure to see what it would earn at the current rate. Useful for a quick calculation before a manual export.
Night tab — overnight arbitrage
Automates the full overnight charge-and-export cycle. It runs entirely on the server, so it works while your phone is off or away from home.
How the cycle works
Phase 1 — Charge
At your start time the Powerwall charges from the grid to your target %. This uses cheap overnight electricity.
Phase 2 — Export
Once charged, the battery discharges and exports to the grid at your Agile export rate. The app tracks the average rate across all slots.
Phase 3 — Recharge
When the battery reaches around 0%, it recharges to 100% from the grid, ready for the day ahead.
Phase 4 — Standby then Normal
Once fully charged the battery waits until your end time, then returns to normal autonomous operation.
Key settings
Charge to % — how full to charge before exporting. 80–90% is recommended. Higher means more energy to sell but a longer charge time.
Start time — set this to when your cheap overnight import rate begins. Common choices: 23:30 (Agile), 00:30 (Octopus Go), 02:00 (Cosy). Check your import tariff.
End time — when the cycle finishes and normal operation resumes. 05:30 works for most tariffs. Set it before your cheap rate window ends.
Car charging control — if your Tesla car charges overnight, enable this. Phase 2 pauses car charging so it doesn't reduce your export, then resumes it in Phase 3.
Holiday tab
Exports during the day while you're away. It runs from 05:30 until your chosen stop time, operating alongside the overnight cycle.
No rate threshold to set — the app automatically identifies the best third of today's remaining Agile slots and only exports during those.
Reserve floor — the app measures your actual standby consumption (fridge, router, etc.) and calculates the minimum battery % needed to power the house until the overnight cycle begins at 23:30. It will never export below this floor.
Enable when you leave, disable when you return. When you are home your consumption is much higher — the app will take a few hours to recalibrate.
Holiday mode and overnight arbitrage work together automatically. Holiday mode handles the day, overnight arbitrage handles the night.
Good practice
Check after your first overnight run. Open the Night tab in the morning — confirm all phases completed and earnings are showing. The phase timeline shows where the cycle got to.
Align your start time with your import tariff. Phase 1 charges from the grid — make sure this happens during your cheapest rate window, not outside it.
Earnings shown are estimates. The app calculates revenue based on battery capacity and Agile rates. Your actual Octopus credit may differ slightly due to meter timing and standing charges.
Don't run manual exports during an overnight cycle. The automated cycle manages export mode itself — using the manual controls on the Home tab at the same time will interfere.
The 5-day chart builds over time. It takes 5 days to become fully representative. During that period it shows however many days of history have been collected.
Troubleshooting
Green dot — connected and reading live data normally.
Amber dot — connecting. If it stays amber, tap Refresh on the Home tab.
Grey dot — not connected. Go to Settings and tap Reconnect Tesla.
Common issues
Overnight cycle didn't start — check the toggle on the Night tab is on and that the start time has passed. The server runs once per minute so there can be up to a 1-minute delay.
Car control not working — tap Find car in Night settings. If still not found, go to accounts.tesla.com → Connected apps → Powerwall Manager and tick vehicle permissions, then reconnect Tesla.
Octopus rates not loading — check your API key and account number in Settings → Octopus Energy. The key should start with sk_live_
Cycle appears stuck in a phase — check the battery % on the Home tab. Phase 2 ends when the battery reaches around 0%; Phase 3 ends when it reaches around 98%.
